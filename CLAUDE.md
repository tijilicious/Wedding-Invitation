# Tijil & Arpita — wedding invitation website

Live: https://tijilicious.github.io/Wedding-Invitation/ (GitHub Pages, branch `claude/website-github-repo-8pfcqb`, root). Wedding: Wednesday 2 December 2026, Golden Stone Resort, Aligarh. Invitation is from the groom's side.

## What this is
One page (`index.html`) plus `media/`. No build step: open `index.html` or serve the folder (`python3 -m http.server`).

## Flow (story mode)
0. Card-opening film (`media/card-open2.mp4`, 10 s, Gemini-generated): a brown handmade-paper card with a gold mandala wax seal on maroon velvet. Shown in an exact 9:16 box (`.film`, container units) so the words sit on the card's inner panel by percentage (panel inside the gold border = x 150–570, y 297–992 of 720×1280; seal centre 360,636). Before the tap: first frame (`card-open.jpg`) with a pulsing ring on the seal, "Tap the seal to open" / "Open without music", personal greeting from `#Gaur-Family` or `?to=Gaur+Family` ("Dear Gaur Family") on the velvet above the card. Tap → song fades in, the film plays: borders glow 0–2 s, seal breaks ~2.5 s (petal burst), flaps open 3–4.5 s; the seal halves leave with the flaps and the centre stays clear. At 6.1 s the card's words fade in (HTML, so Hindi works): ॥ श्री गणेशाय नमः ॥, शुभ विवाह, Tijil with Arpita (Great Vibes script), Wednesday / 2 December 2026, Golden Stone Resort, Aligarh. The film ends on the open card; 3.5 s later (or on tap, "Tap to continue") the intro fades and the story starts underneath. If the film can't play (rejected or no progress in 5 s, or not done in 15 s) the opened card is shown as a still (`card-open2-end.jpg`) with the words; if that still can't load either, it skips straight to the story (the words must never sit on the closed card). The story timer and navigation only run once the story has started (`started`), not during the card film. `card-open.mp4`/`-sd`/`-end.jpg` are kept as copies of the `card-open2` files so a cached older page still finds them. Reduced motion: still card + words straight away. Card only shows when JS runs (`html.js`).
   No `<meta name="viewport">` on the page (never had one): phones lay it out ~980 px wide and scale down; everything scales with `--col`/`cqw`, so intro sizes are pure `cqw` (no px clamps).
1. Boat (`boat.mp4`) — names, TA monogram, date, venue (Golden Stone Resort, Aligarh), countdown to the baraat (2 Dec 2026 18:00 IST).
2. Shiv–Parvati (`shiv-parvati.mp4`) — "Smt Renu Sharma & Shri Atul Sharma request … wedding of their son / TIJIL & ARPITA / daughter of Smt Anjali Sharma & Shri R.K. Sharma, Aligarh". Mothers' names always come first; Arpita is named only once on this page.
3. Radha–Krishna (`radha-krishna.mp4`) — ॥ सखा सप्तपदा भव ॥ (Saptapadi, seventh vow).
4. Celebrations over `haldi.mp4` — one page with all events: Haldi (2 Dec, 12 noon, Calendar), Baraat (2 Dec, 6 PM, Golden Stone Resort, Directions + Calendar), Vadhu Aagman (3 Dec, morning). This page is wrapped in `.fitbox` and scales itself (laid out wider by 1/k, then `scale(k)`, k found by binary search) so its line breaks stay the same while the other pages keep the large global size.
5. Closing over `mandap.mp4` (placeholder until a dedicated closing clip exists) — blessings of Late Shri Ramkishore Sharma & Late Smt Malti Devi; hosts Smt Renu Sharma & Shri Atul Sharma and family (no city).

Not included by request: mandap/tilak, mehendi, sangeet, reception, RSVP, phone numbers, विनीत/बाल आकांक्षा section, "our story" text.

## Behaviour
- Sizing: large text for older guests. Everything is in rem; the root font size is `clamp(22px, col × 0.084, 44px)` (`--col` = phone width, or 9:16 of the screen height on wide screens). Each page then fits itself: `fit()` sets CSS `zoom` on that page's `.over` to the largest value (≤1) at which its text stays clear of the top bar and bottom controls (binary search). Page 3 (Saptapadi) is capped at its earlier size (`capFor`: min(col×0.063, height×0.031), ×1.08 in Hindi). The events page uses `.fitbox` + transform instead. Browsers without `zoom` fall back to shrinking the root. Top bar and bottom controls use clamp() px ranges so they don't grow with the text. Runs on load, font load, resize and language switch. Tap targets keep a 44px minimum. Don't add fixed px font sizes.
- Entrance: after the card film, `begin()` starts the story (boat clip playing) underneath and the intro cross-fades away over 1.2 s. The story clips start downloading only once the card film can play through (or after 4 s), so they don't compete with it.
- Navigation is a fixed stage, not page scrolling: pages are stacked and cross-fade (`.scene.active`). Tap right 65% = next, left 35% = back; swipe up/left = next, down/right = back; wheel/trackpad = one page per gesture (900 ms lock); arrow keys; space = pause. Hold = pause while held. Pause button freezes the clip and the timer. Only the active page's clip plays.
- Autoplays like Instagram stories: `data-dur` seconds per scene (just under each clip's length), progress bars on top, Back / Next (Replay on the last scene) / music at the bottom; tap left third = back, elsewhere = next; hold = pause; arrow keys work.
- iPhone Low Power Mode / data-saver block autoplay even for muted inline video, leaving only the poster frame. The tap that opens the card calls play() on every clip (then pauses the ones not needed), which unlocks them for later programmatic play. If play() is still refused, the clip gets `.kb` (slow drift) so it never sits frozen.
- Every clip loops. Entering a scene restarts its clip only if it is paused. Never reset `src`/`currentTime` or call `load()` on a playing clip (caused restarts). Every clip is downloaded whole in story order into a blob URL; `useLocal()` switches a clip to its blob only while it is paused (on page entry, or right away for pages not on screen), never while playing. Page durations sit inside each clip so autoplay never hits a loop point; the events page plays haldi at `data-rate=0.62` to fill 14.4 s. The last page's bar fills over 10 s and the story rests there (no pause badge).
- Don't transform videos or put `backdrop-filter` over them — both lag on phones. `.ambient` blur is hidden on portrait screens.
- Hindi/English toggle: elements carry `data-hi`; English is captured from innerHTML at load; choice saved in localStorage.
- Music: `media/tum-prem-ho.mp3` ("Tum Prem Ho (Reprise)", supplied by the user): the original file from 43.8 s to 12 s before its end (2 s fade-out), 96 kbps, ~3 MB. Plays from 0 (`SONG_START = 0`) and loops back to the start. It streams normally; only if the seek doesn't take (host without range requests) is it fetched whole into a blob (`songWhole`), so it never competes with the videos for bandwidth on GitHub Pages. Music itself ends ~4:40 (speech after).
- WhatsApp/OG preview: absolute `og:image` = `media/og.jpg` on the Pages URL.

## Media
Every clip has a lighter `*-sd.mp4` (540×960, ~0.8 Mbps cap, ~40% smaller). The page uses them when `navigator.connection` reports save-data / 2g / 3g / downlink < 2.5 Mbps, or when the first clip arrives slower than 3 Mbps in its first ~1.5 s (that probe download is then cancelled and the `-sd` first clip fetched instead); clips not yet downloaded switch to `-sd` at that point. Only the first clip has `autoplay`/`preload=auto`; the rest are `preload=none` and the song is `preload=none` (it starts loading on the opening tap), so nothing competes with the first clip. Any new clip needs both versions.
Gemini-generated, 720×1280, H.264 **Main** profile, ~1.3 Mbps cap, **keyframe every 1 s** (`-g 24 -keyint_min 24 -sc_threshold 0`), no audio, `-movflags +faststart`. Clips with a single keyframe stalled on phones; keep this encoding for any new clip. The story timer pauses while the active clip is `waiting` (buffering). `shiv-parvati` and `haldi` are 9 s with the last second crossfaded into the start (seamless loop). `mandap.mp4` is only the first 10 s of the user's 20 s clip (the user does not want the later horse part), played forward then reversed (20 s seamless loop). `*.jpg` are posters. `card-open2.mp4` plays once (no loop); the Gemini sparkle in its bottom-right corner is patched over with nearby velvet (crop 52×58 at 512,1132 → overlay at 574,1132). Gemini also drew a second, whole seal on the inner panel (centre 351,644) from frame 74 (~3.1 s) to ~5.8 s, visible in the gap between the moving seal halves. It is painted out with clean paper from the last frame: frames 74–83 only between the tracked flap edges (the halves' straight cuts), from frame 84 the whole circle (solid to r 90, soft to r 106), brightness-matched per frame.
